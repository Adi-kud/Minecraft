Minecraft Mobs

Mobs are living creatures in Minecraft that make the world feel alive. They are divided into three main categories: Passive, Neutral, and Hostile. Some mobs help players by providing food or useful items, while others attack and make survival more challenging.

Passive Mobs

  • Cow – Drops beef and leather.
  • Sheep – Provides wool and mutton.
  • Pig – Drops porkchops.
  • Chicken – Lays eggs and drops feathers and chicken.
  • Horse – Can be tamed and ridden.
  • Villager – Trades valuable items with players.

Neutral Mobs

  • Wolf – Can be tamed with bones and becomes a loyal pet.
  • Enderman – Usually peaceful but attacks if looked at directly.
  • Piglin – Lives in the Nether and trades gold with players.
  • Iron Golem – Protects villages and attacks hostile mobs.

Hostile Mobs

  • Zombie – A common enemy that attacks players.
  • Skeleton – Uses a bow and shoots arrows.
  • Creeper – Explodes when it gets close to the player.
  • Spider – Climbs walls and attacks at night.
  • Witch – Throws harmful potions.
  • Slime – Splits into smaller slimes when defeated.

Boss Mobs

  • Ender Dragon – The final boss found in the End dimension.
  • Wither – A powerful boss created by the player using Soul Sand and Wither Skeleton Skulls.

Every mob has its own behavior and abilities. Learning how to interact with different mobs is an important part of surviving and progressing in Minecraft.
